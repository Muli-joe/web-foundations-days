About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple note-taking resource designed to help users keep important information organized and easy to access.

It provides quick instructions, useful features, keyboard shortcuts, and a way for users to send feedback.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open QuickNotes in your web browser.
  2. Choose the information or note you want to view.
  3. Read or use the note to complete your task.
  4. Send feedback if you have a suggestion or problem.

Features

  • Simple and easy-to-use notes
  • Organized information
  • Keyboard shortcuts
  • Feedback form
